---
name: VDACQ funding portfolio language
description: How to describe Cohort Capital and LeaderShield Funding without misrepresenting VDACQ's wider ownership portfolio.
---

Present Cohort Capital and LeaderShield Funding under “Get Funding Options” or equivalent funding-specific language. Do not use “Companies We Own” for a section containing only those two businesses.

**Why:** VDACQ owns Cohort Capital and LeaderShield Funding, but they are not the only companies it owns. A broad ownership heading over only those two implies an incomplete portfolio list.

**How to apply:** Use the two names when explaining growth financing and operational funding. Ownership can be stated accurately in supporting funding copy, but the section itself must remain scoped to funding options.
